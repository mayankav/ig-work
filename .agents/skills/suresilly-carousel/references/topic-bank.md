### How to use

**Layer 1 Suggestion:** When lucky, pull 12-15 candidates: 5 from this bank (filter `Last used` empty or oldest), 5 from VOC table in ledger/research, 2 from pattern gaps (which Tier 1 pattern not used in last 5 decks), 2 from search/trend ("waiting mode" surge, "functional freeze" surge). Do not invent scenes — copy VOC verbatim.

**Layer 2 Selection:** Score each candidate 1-5 on:

1. **Scene** — filmable with body/number/place? (5 = phone face-down + 10 min + chest tight)
2. **Self-blame invertibility** — clear moral failure to absolve? (5 = "I'm lazy" → biology)
3. **Stealability** — one sentence survives alone in a DM? (5 = "Waiting mode isn't laziness")
4. **Freshness** — not in last 10 decks, not same pillar/pattern? (5 = new pillar+pattern)
5. **Intent fit** — matches declared sends (scene) vs saves (reference) need? (5 = perfect)

Total 25. Ship highest ≥18. Below 18 = topic not ready, mine again. Below 15 for all = bank is stale, go mine 10 new VOC.

Log winner in bank `Last used` + `HOOK_LEDGER.md` with scores.

Pillar rotation: never same pillar as last 2 decks. Pattern rotation: never same pattern as last 2 decks (fixes "all brain did it" Hidden Mechanism monotony).
