## Caption

You typed "yes of course!!" with two exclamation points.
Then your stomach dropped.
And you spent the next 72 hours quietly dreading Friday night.

Why do we do this?

When an unexpected invite pops up, our brain's fawn response treats saying "no" as immediate conflict. Saying "yes" buys instant relief in the moment, but mortgages your peace for the rest of the week.

Resentment isn't proof that you're a bad friend.
It's just the quiet tax of an unchosen yes.

Next time you feel the panic reflex:
1. Don't answer on the spot.
2. Buy 10 minutes: "Let me check my week and get back to you by [time]."
3. Offer a counter that fits your actual capacity.

Save slide 8 for the next time someone puts you on the spot.

Send this to the friend who needs permission to say no this weekend.
