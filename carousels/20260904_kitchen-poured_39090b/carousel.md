## Caption
Leaning on the kitchen counter after a simple act, feeling utterly spent, is a common experience, draining more energy than you realize.

This isn't about laziness. It's often mistaken for general fatigue, but it's the specific weariness from a constant flow of minor, unseen efforts that never quite reach a clear end.

This feeling is never a sign of your own fault.

Save this post for the next time you pour a glass of water and feel that sudden wave of tiredness.
