# Six content areas and the weekly plan (owner approved 2026-10-06)

Niche: the first job in India, ages 18 to 30. Mascot: Silly. Reels find strangers; carousels serve followers.

| Area | What it is | Look | Reel form |
|---|---|---|---|
| Know your rights | HR says, law says, with the source | yellow | HR quote, number slam, source clipping, SAY THIS chat |
| Say this | Exact words for a hard moment | ink | chat screen types the reply |
| New rule | What changed, only when a dated source exists | teal | big date, struck number, clipping |
| You asked | Answers to real reader questions | cream | question, chat answer, ASK THIS |
| Office dictionary and A or B | Fun: corporate phrases translated | coral | dictionary entry, stamp |
| You are not behind | One sharp feeling post a week, specific not sappy | cream | slow slams |

A week has 14 slots. About 10 are Reels until sends per reach is measured (our own data: Reels about 143 reach, carousels about 5). Rough mix: 5 inform, 3 help, 2 Q&A, 3 fun, 1 feel.

The last slide or scene of every post: "Follow @suresilly. Got a question? Drop it below. We answer in our next posts." Questions from readers feed You asked. Never send raw reader text to the free model (DPDP Act, Gemini free-tier terms): strip names, employer and pay first.
