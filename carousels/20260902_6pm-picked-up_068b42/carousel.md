## Caption
Exit Block costs you time and plans, blurring your own limits.
This pattern often shows up when you are tired or in a hurry, making it hard to leave without feeling rude. It's not about being impolite, but about setting clear lines.
You are not a failure for finding it hard to leave. This is a common, tricky pattern.
Save this for the next time you feel held by a talk at 6pm, so you can remember your next clear step.
