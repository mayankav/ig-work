# Table 7: White-Space Analysis (v2)

**Methodology:** Whitespace is identified by triangulating three signals:
1. High organic demand (search volume, DM saves, audience intent)
2. Low Instagram carousel saturation (< 3 accounts with 300K+ followers doing this specifically)
3. Platform-native advantage: why Instagram carousels specifically outperform video/LinkedIn/podcast in this space
