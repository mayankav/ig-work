## Caption
Worth Wait is the pattern of seeking outside approval for small errors, costing you your inner calm.

This often shows up as a slight pause, a moment of holding your breath, or an urge to over-explain a minor slip-up, even when no one else has noticed. It's often seen as good manners or being modest, but it's really a habit of giving your power away.

This isn't a failure of kindness or good manners.

Save this for the next time you find yourself waiting for a nod, when all you need is your own steady peace.
