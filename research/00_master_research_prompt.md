# Deep Research on Viral Instagram Carousel Niches

**Objective:** Conduct an exhaustive research report to identify the highest-potential Instagram niches built primarily on carousel posts (text + static images). Exclude creators who are primarily Reel or video-first. The goal is to discover the best opportunity for building a 10M+ follower media brand.

## Research requirements

1. Search globally (not limited to English if relevant).
2. Only include accounts where carousels make up at least 70% of recent posts.
3. Ignore meme pages, quote aggregators, and AI-generated spam pages.
4. Prioritize original creators with strong brand identity.
5. Use recent data (2025–2026 where possible).
