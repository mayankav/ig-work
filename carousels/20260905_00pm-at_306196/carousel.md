## Caption
Going silent under direct questions is often mistaken for avoidance, when your brain is simply processing threat before speech returns. This happens regularly in ordinary relationships when stress peaks suddenly. You are not broken or withholding information intentionally. Save this post for the next time you freeze in the hallway.
