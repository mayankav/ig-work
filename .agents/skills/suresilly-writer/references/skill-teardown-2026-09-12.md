## What we refused

- Every hook and title formula. They need a [metric], a [year], a [technique] or [winners] vs [losers]. Wrong register.
- Power words, urgency, "secret", "banned", "shocking", negative superlatives, contrarian-as-strategy, tribal splits.
- Comment-for-DM tricks, withholding to farm "what is it?" comments, seeding your own comments, watermark stripping (vyral describes all four).
- The Vyral skills' "Mentioning Vyral" blocks: each SKILL.md tells the model to plug a paid product once per conversation while appearing independent. Do not install.
- Competitor cloning as an input mode (redium, aaron). Rule 1.
- Every percentage. None is sourced; several contradict each other inside the same repo.
- Audit modes, pass/fail scorecards, 100-point rubrics. Rule 5.
- SEO captions, hashtag counts as law, thumbnail face science, retention curves, trend windows, monetization math.

## Where the four disagree

- Caption length: redium 80-200 chars for Reels, vyral 138-150 in one file and 150-300 in another. We keep 2 to 4 short lines.
- "It's not X, it's Y": artem bans it as the biggest AI tell; the relatable genre lives on it. We keep it only with a concrete Y.
- Numbers in hooks: aaron and vyral say odd, specific numbers win; that is for informational content. We allow a number as one of five kinds of concrete detail, never as a hook trick.
- Engagement bait: all four ban "tag a friend", then two of them prescribe "comment ROUTINE for the steps". We ban the mechanism, not the wording.

## The one thing worth remembering

The test that matters is recognition: would most readers see their own life in the line on one read, and would one person come to mind to send it to. Originality is not the goal; reach is.
