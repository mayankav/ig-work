# Table 1: Niche Opportunity Ranking (v2)

## Scoring Methodology

Each niche is scored 1–10 across 7 dimensions. The Overall Score is a **weighted average** using the following weights, calibrated for the goal of a 10M+ carousel-first brand:

| Dimension | Weight | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Virality Score | 20% | Content must travel organically without paid spend |
| Shareability | 20% | Shares are the #1 growth mechanism for carousel accounts |
| Save Rate | 15% | Algorithm boost; correlates with evergreen content quality |
| Audience Size | 15% | TAM ceiling determines the 10M follower ceiling |
| Monetization | 15% | Validates long-term commercial viability |
| Evergreen | 10% | Trend-dependent niches risk collapse (see: Crypto 2022) |
| Competition | 5% | Inverted score — higher competition = lower score; low weight because differentiation beats saturation |

**Engagement benchmarks used (2025 Instagram carousel averages by account size):**
- < 100K followers: 4–7% engagement rate
- 100K–500K: 2–4%
- 500K–2M: 1–2.5%
- > 2M: 0.8–1.5%
