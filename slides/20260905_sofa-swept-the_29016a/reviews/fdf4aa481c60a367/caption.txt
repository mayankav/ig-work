Small household jobs have a way of lingering in plain sight while the evening slips past unnoticed. We tell ourselves we will finish them in a moment, but the physical objects in our line of sight pull our attention in very different directions. This pattern is not a failure of willpower or personal discipline. Save this post for the next time you find yourself staring at half finished chores while the clock moves toward eight.
