## Caption

You sent a lively 3-sentence update.
They replied: "okay."
With a period.
And your stomach instantly dropped.

Why does a neutral text feel like a slammed door?

In digital communication, flat text strips away facial expressions, eye contact, and tone of voice. When your nervous system learned early on that subtle mood shifts meant impending conflict, it treats ambiguity as an active threat.

Your brain isn't overreacting for fun. It's predicting danger to protect you from being caught off guard.

Next time a dry text triggers the alarm:
1. Put the phone face-down for 90 seconds.
2. Remember that flat punctuation usually means busy, not angry.
3. Use the gentle story-check script from Slide 5 instead of spiraling.

Save slide 8 for the next time a period sends you into an overthinking loop.

Send this to the friend who rereads every text four times.
