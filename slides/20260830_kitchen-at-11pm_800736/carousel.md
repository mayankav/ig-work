## Caption
You stood in the kitchen at 11pm and washed heavy ceramic bowls by hand because it felt safer than sitting down. Forty minutes spent scrubbing things that were not dirty to earn a quiet room where nobody needed anything from you. Walker found that appeasing is learned where it worked, which is why it arrives before you have decided anything. We call this preemptive appeasement, keeping the peace before a sound is even made. The work does not finish itself just because you stay up. Say the words out loud when the bowl is done, turn off the light, and leave the room.
