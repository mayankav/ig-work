## Caption
Transition Freeze is the quiet cost of a day that refuses to end. It happens when your body stays sat on the bed long after your mind has finished the work, leaving you stranded in the hallway of your own home.

This is a common friction between wanting to move and the physical weight of the space you occupy. It is often mistaken for a lack of discipline, but it is simply a failure of the bridge between intention and action.

This is not a character flaw and it was never a sign of laziness.

Save this for the next 11:45pm you spend staring at the wall.
