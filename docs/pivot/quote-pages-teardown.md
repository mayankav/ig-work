# Quote-page teardown and new plan

Collected 2026-09-11 from logged-out Instagram pages. For each profile: follower count, bio, and the last 12 grid posts, each with its type, like count, comment count, date, caption, and the text Instagram read from the image. 84 posts in total. Where a like count says "hidden", the owner has hidden it.

---

## The short version

1. **The following comes from a few big hits, not from the typical post.** Pages with about 2M followers usually get 1K–7K likes a post (0.1–0.4% of followers). Then one post gets 94K–364K. Growth is a lottery you buy many tickets for.
2. **Every hit makes the reader think of one person and send it to them:** an old school friend, a partner, someone you are glad you met. A DM send is how a post reaches people who don't follow you.
3. **Each page has one look and never changes it.** One paper colour, one font, one small drawing or character, one signature. People recognise the page before they read it, and each post takes minutes to make.
4. **The writing needs nothing proved.** Simple, universal lines in the second person about love, friendship, growing up, parents, nostalgia, adult life and being kind to yourself. No sources and no expert claims.
5. **Big hits are long carousels.** poetsphere's two biggest posts in this sample have 15 and 18 slides. None of the 84 posts is a Reel. This genre wins on still images.

Our baseline: the only post of ours with measured insights (2026-09-01) **reached 2 accounts**, with 0 saves and 0 shares. That came from ~24K lines of Python and 75 test files.

## Why each page works

### poet.circle: 2.3M, "The #1 Self-Empowerment Page"
- **Look:** a flat solid colour (red, dark green, mustard, brown, black), a small handwriting-style font, and a tiny "-PC" signature in the corner. Every tile looks the same.
- **Biggest hit (364K likes, 13K comments):** a single image saying that a friend who has lasted since middle school is rare. The 13K comments are mostly people tagging that friend. The caption is two words.
- **Second (36K likes, 3,421 comments):** a carousel prayer whose caption asks you to say it out loud. The comments are people typing "Amen".
- **Series:** a dated "Daily Feeling" card, for a daily habit.
- **Captions:** 1–4 words ("Agree?", "Drop a ❤️"). The image does all the work.
- **Money:** a link-in-bio product, a newsletter, and paid book promos.
- [The post you linked](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdBsNdtgTFV/) is a 7-slide story on kraft paper. Slide 1 poses a question and ends with it unanswered, so you have to swipe. Slides 2–3 pass months and build tension. Slides 4–6 answer it with a line worth saving. The caption is the paid book plug. It got 14.9K likes.

### poetsphere: 1.7M, "Poetry · Quotes · Relatable"
The best model for us.
- **Look:** cream paper, lowercase handwriting, a tiny stick-figure doodle, lots of empty space. Now and then a dark red or blue card.
- **[230K-like post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcguPuZCG2d/), 18 slides:** a list of small life lessons, each credited to an invented child ("—name, age 7"). Every slide stands alone and can be screenshotted.
- **[94K-like post](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc6cajSge4B/), 15 slides:** one thought spread over 15 slides, about how many small accidents had to happen for you to meet someone you love. Each slide is one beat with a doodle. The caption ends by telling you to send it to whoever came to mind and to save it. 895 comments, mostly tags.
- **One slide literally says** "send this to the first person you think of".
- **Pins its 3 biggest hits** so a new visitor sees them first.
- **Money:** a book affiliate link in the bio, plus paid promos.

### best.memory__: 2.1M, "Motivation | Positive | Quotes"
- **Look:** a photo of an open book or paper page, typewriter serif text, yellow or pink highlighter over the key phrase.
- **Series:** a numbered "Day N of 365" card, which gives people a reason to come back.
- **Parenting lists:** "Your son/daughter will meet people who… teach them these 7 rules." These get saved by parents and forwarded to partners.
- **Caption CTAs spell out the action:** "Save this if…" and "Tag your ___ person."
- **Follow bait:** many carousels include a slide telling you that if you don't follow, you'll never see this again.
- **Cross-promotes** a sister page in the bio.

### black_thoughtss_: 2.1M
- **Look:** painterly storybook illustrations (AI-generated in style) with short white typewriter text on the image. Couples in the rain, cats by a lake, a lamb, an elephant.
- **Formats:**
  - *Two-line contrast:* what a careless person says vs. what the right person says.
  - *Fable with a question hook:* "why does [animal] do [thing]?", answered as a short story ([21K likes](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdDYevdGlxc/)).
  - *Childhood memories* and *faith*.
- **All carousels,** about 4–6 a day.
- **Captions:** a long reflective paragraph, usually opening with "Sometimes…".

### sasuke.japanese01: 198K, "Japanese Wisdom"
- **Look:** Japanese ink-wash landscapes, a lone figure, a red sun. The cover template never changes: *"One Japanese [tea master / gardener / fisherman] once said…"* or *"3 Japanese ideas about…"*.
- **It works because** one fixed frame ("Japanese wisdom") makes an ordinary line feel profound, and the frame makes the page instantly recognisable.
- **Every caption is a sales pitch** (e-book, journal, newsletter). The carousel is the free sample; the caption is the funnel.
- **Comments are inflated** by a "comment KAIZEN and I'll DM you" automation (1,351 comments on one post). Comments are a ranking signal.
- **Runs sister pages** in the same niche.

### me_and_my_old_monk: 59.6K, the closest to us
- **Look:** the strongest brand system in the set. Every tile is mustard yellow, with **the same recurring character** (the founder's illustrated avatar) in Indian scenes (chai, cassette player, cricket, auto-rickshaw, an 80s café), a header band with the page name, and one line of text.
- **Topics:** adult life, overthinking, 90s nostalgia, quiet grief, parents' trust. A little Hinglish.
- **Best posts:** an adulting Q&A joke (4.1K) and a "90s kids, how many of these characters do you remember?" quiz carousel (45 comments, since quizzes pull answers).
- **Proof** that a mascot character plus one colour world builds recognition. We already have a mascot.

## Hook shapes that repeat across pages

Written as templates, not copied lines:

1. **List of small truths from an unexpected source:** children, grandparents, a Japanese master. The source frame makes a plain line feel wise.
2. **"Think about how unlikely X was":** makes the reader picture a specific person, and then send it to them.
3. **"Imagine having [a rare, lasting good thing]. That's rare.":** a tag magnet.
4. **An open-loop story:** slide 1 asks a question and stops, and the answer comes on slide 4 or later.
5. **Why does [animal] do [thing]?:** a fable with a short lesson at the end.
6. **"Your son/daughter will meet… teach them these N rules":** saved by parents.
7. **"Things I never say out loud…":** a confession list.
8. **"90s kids, how many of these do you remember?":** a nostalgia quiz that pulls comments.
9. **"Day N of 365" / a dated daily card:** a habit series.

## New plan for main (built 2026-09-11)

**Decided:** keep the donkey, write in English, rebuild main. The old engine is frozen on `obsolete/carousel-engine-v1`. How the new pipeline runs is in `AGENTS.md`.

### What we post

One look, three formats.

| Format | When | Slides | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **List of small truths** | mornings, every other day | 7–9 | saves and sends |
| **Open-loop micro-story** | mornings, the other days | 6–8 | time spent and sends |
| **"Send this" one-liner** | every evening | 1 | tags and sends; cheap |

- **Lists stop at 9 slides, not 15.** Telegram's `redo images` reply takes slide numbers 1–9, and the Instagram API documents a limit of 10 images per carousel. We can raise it later if a probe shows the API takes more.
- **The writer draws a shape** (for example "what someone taught you without ever saying it") **and a person** (mum, a school friend, a partner). It writes a draft, then an editor pass tightens it.
- **Every caption ends with one plain action** (send, save or tag), followed by a single line of hashtags.
- **Pin our three biggest posts** once we have them.

### The look
- Warm cream paper with fine grain.
- Fraunces, a soft serif, in lowercase.
- One phrase per slide highlighted in yellow, on no more than 3 slides per post.
- The donkey in the bottom-right corner, with its pose picked from the slide's mood. On a carousel's cover, it points you into the swipe.
- A small `@suresilly` signature, and a slide counter on carousels.

### Cadence
- **Now:** the existing 08:00 and 20:00 IST clock, with one post per slot.
- **Later:** add a third slot once the pipeline is stable. That needs a Worker change and a `wrangler deploy`.

### Loose rules
These replace the old 29 invariants. They are in `AGENTS.md`.
1. Our own words only.
2. The donkey comes from the library, with no text in the art.
3. Every Telegram message says what happened, what you can reply, and what silence does.
4. Kill switch.
5. Keep it small.

### Telegram approval
The flow is the same as before, with better messages:
- **The preview:** the real slides arrive as a swipeable album, followed by a card with the hook, all slide texts (expandable), the caption and the reply options.
- **Replies:** `approve`, `disapprove`, `redo all`, or `redo images 2,4` (new donkey poses on those slides).
- **Silence:** after 1 hour the post goes out by itself.
- **Afterwards:** confirmations, the Instagram link, failures and `retry` all use the same what / reply / silence format.

### How we judge it
- Measure **sends and saves per reach**, not followers. `scripts/insights.py` records both, 3 days after posting.
- **After 60 posts** (about 4 weeks at 2 a day), shift the mix toward whichever format wins on sends per reach.

| best.memory__ | 2.1M | 4-5 a day, "Day N of 365" series, repeated follow-us posts | 3K-16K | volume and a numbered series |

What to take (formats, never words):

1. **Pain first, comfort second.** therelatablewall's best shape: "not everyone has X" then "some people learned to Y". Our lists start soft; a line that names the hard part first hits harder. Fits the evening one-liner.
2. **A named series with a fixed opener.** "Day 255 of 365", "Detachment means...". Cheap recognition. Ours could be a fixed opener per weekday.
3. **A line with a turn.** hugmymind's lines end on a reversal. Our one-liner rule already asks for it; the lists do not. Add it to the second-last list item.
4. **Ask inside the image, not only the caption.** spiritualgoal puts "save this" and "leave a heart" on the slide. Our last slide already says "send this to"; add "save this" on lists.
5. **The caption can carry the story.** hustlingtortoise writes 150 words under a one-line image and gets comments. Our captions are 2-4 lines by rule; a story format could keep a longer caption.

What not to take: numbered lists of 50, mindset comparisons, follow-us posts, and anything argued in the comments.
