## Caption
Agreement hangover is the quiet tax you pay for avoiding friction. It costs you your evenings and eventually your rest. This is not a discipline problem and it was never laziness. It is a reflex that learned to protect you by making you invisible. We mistake this for being helpful or easygoing, but it is actually a form of self-abandonment that leaves you staring at the ceiling when you should be dreaming. Save this for the next 11:45pm you spend wondering why you agreed to something you did not want to do.
