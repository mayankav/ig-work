## Part 1 · Brand Identity & Positioning

### 1.1 What @suresilly Is

**One-liner:** The psychology of why you're like this.

**Positioning statement:** @suresilly is applied behavioral science for modern
adults — less healing, more understanding. It owns the gap between academic
psychology and generic self-help quotes. It's the page you send to your partner
at 11pm with the caption "babe read slide 4."

**Tagline options (use interchangeably):**
- *"making sense of the silly things we do"*
- *"the psychology of why you're like this"*
- *"sure, silly — but also very real"*

### 1.2 What @suresilly Is NOT

| ❌ We are NOT | ✅ We ARE |
|---|---|
| A therapist's page (clinical, careful, liability-conscious) | A smart friend who reads the textbooks so you don't have to |
| Generic quote cards or Canva templates | A visual magazine with a proprietary design language |
| Self-help platitudes ("just communicate!") | Specific scripts, frameworks, and mechanisms |
| Preachy or condescending | Self-aware, witty, occasionally self-deprecating |
| Trauma-dumping or triggering without purpose | Honest about hard truths but always landing on agency |

### 1.3 Target Audience

- **Primary:** Adults 25–38, relationship-aware, psychologically curious
- **Psychographic:** People who follow both Tim Ferriss AND Esther Perel
- **Gender:** Skews 60/40 female/male but content is gender-neutral
- **Behavioral signal:** They've been to therapy at least once, or listen to
  psychology podcasts, or have googled "anxious attachment style"
- **Platform behavior:** Heavy savers, frequent DM-sharers, moderate commenters

### 1.4 The @suresilly Voice

Every word of copy must pass through these voice dials:

| Dial | Setting | What It Sounds Like |
|---|---|---|
| **Warmth** | 8/10 | Compassionate, validating, non-judgmental. Never cold or clinical. |
| **Intellectual sharpness** | 9/10 | Psychologically accurate, well-sourced, rigorous. Never dumbed-down. |
| **Self-deprecation** | 6/10 | Lighthearted, relatable group-chat energy. "we're all a little cooked." |
| **Clinical jargon** | 1/10 | Translate textbook psychology into everyday human English. Say "shutting down" not "deactivating attachment strategies." |
| **Humor register** | Dry/deadpan | Observational, never slapstick. Think: "ah yes, the classic 'I'm fine' followed by 4 hours of silence." |
| **Authority posture** | Peer, not guru | We're figuring this out together, but we've done the reading. |

**Voice test:** Before finalizing any slide, ask: *"Would a 28-year-old send
this slide to their best friend in a group chat at midnight?"* If not, rewrite.

### 2.3 Content Pillars (The 3 Post Types)

**Cadence: two carousels a day, every day. Carousels only — there are no Reels.**
`.github/workflows/auto-post.yml` runs `run.py` at 08:00 and 20:00 IST, one deck
each. The two are spaced far apart on purpose: Instagram balances how much it
shows from one account, so two posts close together compete with each other.

The three pillars below are the *kinds* of deck, not a weekly timetable. Nothing
assigns a pillar to a weekday — `run.py` picks the moment, and the pattern comes
from rotation against the last few decks (see "Rotate the pattern" in
`content-playbook.md`). A deck records which pillar it landed on in its
`**Content Pillar:**` line.

1. **Diagnosis Post**: Names a pattern the reader recognizes but couldn't
   articulate. *"5 signs you're the 'therapist friend' and it's burning you out."*
2. **Script / Template Post**: Gives the reader exact words to use.
   *"Instead of 'it's fine', say 'I need a minute to figure out what I'm
   actually feeling.' Here are 6 more."*
3. **Philosophy / Reframe Post**: A counter-intuitive observation that
   reframes how the reader sees a common situation. *"You don't have commitment
   issues. You have trust issues. The difference matters."*
