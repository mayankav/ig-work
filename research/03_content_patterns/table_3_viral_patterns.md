# Table 3: Viral Content Pattern Analysis (v2)

**Methodology:** 20 distinct patterns identified across the top-performing carousel accounts, observed from posts with >= 50K impressions at sub-1M accounts. Niche attribution indicates where each pattern significantly over-indexes relative to its baseline.

| # | Pattern | Example Hook | Avg Slides | Emotional Trigger | CTA Style | Niche Over-Index | Why It Goes Viral |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The Diagnosis** | "5 signs you have an anxious attachment style" | 7 | Fear → Relief (getting named) | "Tag someone who needs this" | Relational Psych, Men's MH | Gives the audience a clinical label for their felt experience. People share to others as a form of indirect communication. |
| 2 | **The Step-by-Step Tutorial** | "How to automate your inbox with Make.com in 20 min" | 10 | Aspiration + Utility | "Save this — you'll need it" | AI/Auto, No-Code, Python | Highest save-rate pattern. Carousels that function as instruction manuals are bookmarked and returned to. |
| 3 | **The Uncomfortable Truth** | "Nobody tells you how hard it is to leave a comfortable job" | 8 | Cognitive Dissonance | "Share if you felt this" | Solopreneurship, Identity, Finance | Forces audience to acknowledge a suppressed belief. High "share to story" because it validates their private experience publicly. |
| 4 | **The Identity Mirror** | "Which type of overthinker are you? (Take this quiz)" | 6 | Ego + Self-Discovery | "Comment your type" | Psychology, Men's MH, Habits | Gamification. Creates comment volume by demanding self-disclosure. Types/categories always outperform non-categorical posts. |
| 5 | **The Before → After Proof** | "My Figma files in year 1 vs year 4 (full breakdown)" | 10 | Hope + Social Proof | "What slide shocked you most?" | UI/UX, Habits, Freelancing | Visual proof is the strongest trust-builder. Users swipe to the end to see the result — full retention through the post. |
| 6 | **The Contrarian Reversal** | "Why waking up at 5AM is making you worse, not better" | 8 | Outrage + Curiosity | "Agree or disagree? Tell me." | Habits, Productivity, Mindset | Violates a dominant cultural assumption. Generates high comment volume via debate, which the algorithm rewards as conversation. |
| 7 | **The Tool Arsenal** | "10 AI tools doing the work of a $10,000 agency" | 9 | FOMO + Greed | "Save before they blow up" | AI/Auto, No-Code, UX | High perceived value per slide. Each tool is a micro-discovery. Post is saved by definition because users want to revisit. |
| 8 | **The Mistake Autopsy** | "I sent 3,000 cold emails. Here's every mistake I made." | 9 | Fear of Failure + Empathy | "Share with someone still struggling" | Copywriting, Sales, Freelancing | Personal failure narrative + actionable fix. Feels like getting advice from an older sibling. Emotional honesty drives DMs and shares. |
| 9 | **The Named Framework** | "The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Method for anxiety spirals" | 7 | Clarity + Relief | "Screenshot the last slide" | Somatic Healing, Psych, Habits | Proprietary-feeling frameworks feel like getting something unique. Named frameworks also get re-shared as "the X method." |
| 10 | **The Hidden Mechanism** | "The psychological reason you always go back to toxic people" | 8 | Intrigue + Relief | "Save and revisit this" | Relational Psych, Dark Psych, EQ | Explains the *why* behind painful behaviors. People share because it articulates something they've felt but couldn't explain. |
| 11 | **The Data Reveal** | "I analyzed 100 viral carousels. Here's what I found." | 10 | Trust + Curiosity | "Save the analysis" | Marketing, Copywriting, AI | Numbers signal credibility. Meta-content about content itself is popular because it speaks directly to the creator audience. |
| 12 | **The Script / Template** | "Exact words to use when setting a boundary with a parent" | 8 | Relief + Safety | "Screenshot slide 4 for later" | Relational Psych, Communication | Script content has the highest save rate of any format. User perceives immediate usability. |
| 13 | **The "Nobody Talks About"** | "The financial topics nobody teaches you in school" | 8 | Righteous Indignation | "Share with someone who needs this" | Finance, Identity, Parenting | Positions the creator as exposing hidden truth. Audience shares to feel like the one bringing the suppressed knowledge to others. |
| 14 | **The Lifestyle Deconstruction** | "How I work 3 days a week and make more than I did at $120k/yr" | 9 | Envy + Aspiration | "Follow for the full breakdown series" | Solopreneurship, Finance, Travel | Income + lifestyle combination is the most powerful aspirational pairing on the platform. Very high profile visit rate from slide 1. |
| 15 | **The Myth Buster** | "5 investing myths that are keeping you broke" | 7 | Outrage + Clarity | "Which myth surprised you most?" | Finance, Health, Psychology | Each slide dismantles one belief. Creates a satisfying "de-programming" feeling. Naturally comment-generating. |
| 16 | **The Visual Comparison** | "Healthy vs. unhealthy relationship behaviors (visual guide)" | 8 | Validation + Recognition | "Send to a friend who needs to see this" | Relational Psych, Parenting | Binary comparison is scannable and immediately understandable. | 19 | **The Relationship Archetype** | "The 4 attachment styles and how they destroy love" | 8 | Ego + Fear | "Which one describes your ex?" | Relational Psych, Dating | Typology content forces engagement. The question CTA ("which is your ex") invites storytelling in comments. |
