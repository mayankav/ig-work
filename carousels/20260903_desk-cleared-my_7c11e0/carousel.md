## Caption
The Review loop costs you a second shift paid in quiet exhaustion. People often mistake this nightly habit for care and grit, keeping you chained long after the office is empty. You are not failing anyone by walking away when the clock turns. Save this post for the next time you find yourself staring at a dark screen and turning every single sentence over in your head.
